AKU Integrated Disaster Preparedness & Response Program

A 2024 healthcare-worker training program designed to strengthen the knowledge and practical skills of physicians, nurses, and other district healthcare workers responding to disaster-affected populations. The curriculum combines clinical teaching with hands-on activities, scenario-based learning, and structured evaluation.

View AKU Disaster Response Initiative Back to Projects

Program in brief

The program focuses on healthcare needs during disasters and displacement, with training across trauma care, communicable and non-communicable diseases, pediatric emergencies, psychological first aid, nursing care, gender-based violence, adolescent health, and maternal and gynecological emergencies.

Program at a glance

Duration4.5 days, approximately 6 hours per day
Participants25–30 healthcare workers per session
AudienceDistrict physicians, nurses, and dispensers
FormatSmall-group discussion, case-based teaching, videos, role play, and hands-on activities
AssessmentPre/post knowledge testing, participant feedback, and instructor evaluation

My contribution

Supported healthcare-worker training, data collection, pre/post evaluation, data validation, analysis, reporting, research coordination, and study documentation as part of the program.

CURRICULUM AREAS

Training focus

The curriculum was structured around practical healthcare challenges likely to arise in disaster settings, combining didactic learning with hands-on clinical and emergency-response activities.

Trauma & Mass Casualty

ABCDE trauma assessment, airway and cervical-spine management, hemorrhage control, injuries, triage, and disaster drills.

Adult Acute & Chronic Care

Communicable diseases, common infections, asthma, hypertension, diabetes, skin conditions, hands-only CPR, and Stop the Bleed.

Pediatrics & Psychological First Aid

Pediatric illness, malnutrition, neonatal emergencies, child abuse, self-care, grounding techniques, and psychological first aid.

Nursing & Gender-Based Violence

Medication, wound and fracture-management skills, resilience, GBV screening and referral, and adolescent health.

Maternal & Gynecological Emergencies

Disaster essential kits, care for pregnant and non-pregnant women, and maternal and newborn care.

Evaluation & Quality Improvement

Formative assessment, scenario-based skills evaluation, pre/post knowledge assessment, feedback, and instructor evaluation.
